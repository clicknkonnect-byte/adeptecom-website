Questions

Good to know

Is connecting the API secure?
Yes. We use each platform’s official API authorisation flow — you approve access, nothing is scraped, and access can be revoked at any time.
Do the AI agents run everything on their own?
No. Agents do the heavy lifting — monitoring, research, and surfacing what’s working — but a human operator reviews and approves the decisions that actually change your account.
Which platforms can you connect?
TikTok Shop, Amazon, Meta, Google Ads and Merchant Center, Search Console, Shopify, and eBay. If you sell on it, we can usually plug into it.
What should I ask before hiring an "AI-run" agency?
Ask exactly which tasks the AI handles versus what a human still approves, and ask to see one real blended-return report. See our full breakdown in what to actually expect from an AI marketing automation agency.
