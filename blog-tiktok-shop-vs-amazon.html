TikTok Shop vs Amazon: where should a new brand start?

Strategy12 August 2026·6 min read

It’s the question almost every new e-commerce founder asks: launch on TikTok Shop or Amazon first? Both are enormous, but they work in opposite ways — and starting on the wrong one for your product can burn months of cash. Here’s a practical way to decide.

TikTok Shop is discovery-led

On TikTok Shop, nobody was looking for you. A creator’s video stops the scroll, the product looks irresistible, and the sale happens on impulse — often within seconds. It rewards brands that can produce (or activate creators to produce) content that demonstrates a product.

  • Best for: visual, demo-able, impulse-friendly products — beauty, skincare, gadgets, home, supplements.
  • Growth engine: a creator/affiliate network + Spark Ads on the videos that already convert.
  • Watch out for: you need a steady flow of content, and margins have to survive creator commissions.

Amazon is intent-led

On Amazon, the customer is already in buying mode — they typed exactly what they want into the search bar. You don’t create demand; you capture it. Winning is about ranking for the right keywords, a listing that converts, reviews, and disciplined PPC.

  • Best for: products people actively search for, replenishables, and categories with clear existing demand.
  • Growth engine: optimised listings + A+ content + Sponsored Ads, compounding with reviews.
  • Watch out for: competition is fierce, and a weak listing or thin reviews will sink even a great product.
Quick test: if your product needs to be shown to be wanted, lean TikTok Shop. If people already search for it by name or category, lean Amazon.

How to actually choose

Run your product through four filters:

  • Content-ability: can it be demonstrated in a 15-second video? → TikTok Shop.
  • Existing demand: are people already searching for it? → Amazon.
  • Margins: TikTok needs room for creator commissions and ad spend; Amazon needs room for fees and PPC. Know your true unit economics before you pick.
  • Cash flow: TikTok can spike fast (great, but inventory-hungry); Amazon builds more steadily as rankings and reviews compound.

The honest answer: they feed each other

Pick the one that fits your product to start — but the brands that win big rarely stop at one. TikTok Shop creates demand and social proof; that same buzz lifts your branded searches on Amazon. Amazon captures the shoppers who saw you on TikTok but prefer to buy where they already have Prime. Run as one system, each channel makes the other cheaper and stronger.
